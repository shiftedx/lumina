# Lumina navigation artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool for the native iOS Home, Library, Requests and Account tabs; the original Video, Audio and Books variants are retained as sources. These are original Lumina assets, not Netflix artwork. The selected high-resolution transparent PNG sources live beside this file. The iOS asset catalog contains aspect-preserving 32-point canvases with 28-point artwork at 1×, 2× and 3× with template rendering, allowing the system to apply accessible foreground and selected accent colors. Transparent source padding is normalized during export so the symbols have consistent visual size.

Final prompt direction: one pristine pure-black flat glyph on a transparent square canvas, medium smooth rounded contours, generous negative space, crisp at 28 points, approximately 12% transparent margin. No lettering, tile, glow, shadow, texture or bevel. Companion icons use the Home source as a style reference.

- Home: welcoming house outline, open rounded doorway, rising sun and three rays.
- Library: staggered cover cards with a centered play triangle.
- Requests: cinema ticket outline with a four-point discovery star.
- Video: rounded cinema frame, broad film perforations and centered play triangle.
- Audio: vinyl record, broad center opening, two curved groove accents and sweeping tonearm.
- Books: open book, arcing pages, central spine and softly rounded corners.
- Account: round profile halo, simple head and curved shoulders, small opening at upper right.

Native tab titles remain the accessibility labels. The images supply visual identity only; system tab placement, hit areas and navigation remain native.
