## Product Purpose

Lumina is a self-hosted household streaming service. It combines a local media library, online media, downloads, discovery, and household requests. Success means members can find and watch media easily, while owners can manage the server without losing access to its capabilities.

## Design Principles

1. Keep media content central; header navigation opens beside its trigger and leaves the content area available.
2. Separate personal preferences from household-wide administration.
3. Reduce unnecessary complexity through grouping and progressive disclosure while retaining every feature.
4. Give equivalent actions consistent, familiar controls and readable labels.
5. Evaluate each view with actual visual and interaction evidence; verify improvements at desktop and phone sizes.
6. Keep ordinary actions and content groups free of decorative outline frames. Preserve visible keyboard focus, useful dividers, input affordances, and clear selected states.
