# Rating source marks

`metacritic.svg` was copied on 2026-10-28 from the [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) `develop` asset:

- [`icons/metacritic.svg`](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/metacritic.svg)

`imdb.svg` is the unmodified black-on-yellow [IMDb Logo Rectangle](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IMDb_Logo_Rectangle.svg), retrieved 2026-10-28. Wikimedia records IMDb as its author and the IMDb Pressroom brand guidelines as its source. It replaces the monochrome Simple Icons mark so the letters remain legible on light surfaces.

`rottentomatoes-fresh.svg` and `rottentomatoes-rotten.svg` are the unmodified positive and negative score vectors from Rotten Tomatoes' production [`score-icon-critics` component](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/assets/pizza-pie/javascripts/bundles/roma/default.45dc58bf8f0.js), retrieved on 2026-10-28. Their standalone SVG wrappers supply the source component's view box; the vectors, fills, and geometry are unchanged. They are selected only from the Tomatometer score: Fresh at 60% or above, otherwise Rotten. Lumina never infers Certified Fresh.

Metascore badges use Metacritic's documented movie, TV, and music bands and official colors: Favorable 61–100 (`#66CC33`), Mixed 40–60 (`#FFCC33`), and Unfavorable 0–39 (`#FF0000`). These values appear in the current [Metacritic Support table](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15456077802647-What-s-with-these-green-yellow-and-red-colors), whose [published graphic](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/15456061354135) supplies the color values. Lumina does not apply the different game thresholds.

Metacritic retains its black source mark and is inverted only on Lumina's dark surface for legibility.
