Lumina native cinema beta — iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV

Connect to your existing Lumina household server with a member account. The server's connected-app API must be enabled. Allow local-network access for a LAN server. No library or server credentials are bundled with the app.

Please test:
• Featured artwork and poster browsing: selection, title legibility, loading, scrolling, and rotation on iPhone/iPad.
• iOS: a short downward pull reveals search; a deeper pull refreshes the library. Cancel closes search. Account contains Sign Out.
• Apple TV: remote focus, featured selection, poster grids, Details, and the Account dialog.
• Direct playback and “Convert to compatible video”: start, seek, pause, dismiss, relaunch, and synced resume between devices.
• Empty libraries, unavailable server, invalid credentials, and recovery after a connection returns.
• On each device: background the app and return during playback, then check audio/video state and resume position.

Known issue: on tvOS, returning after pressing Home can resume playback without explicitly pressing Play. This beta does not establish HDR, Dolby Vision, Atmos, or HDMI output fidelity.

For feedback, send a screenshot or TestFlight feedback with device/OS, steps, expected behavior, actual behavior, and whether playback used direct or compatible video. Avoid including server credentials or private library details.
